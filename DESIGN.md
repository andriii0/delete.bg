# Light photo studio

Use a warm white surface, charcoal lettering and a vermilion accent for the primary action. Set the wordmark and display type in self-hosted Bricolage Grotesque. The landing page pairs a single upload control with a draggable comparison made from the real gerbera export. On phones the upload control comes first. In the editor, reserve the largest uninterrupted area for the photo and use a neutral checkerboard to judge transparency.
